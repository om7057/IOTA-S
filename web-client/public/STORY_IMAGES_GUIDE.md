# "Arav and the Stranger Offer" - Complete Story Image Guide

## Story Overview
This is a complete, branching narrative (6 scenes) demonstrating child safety, personal boundaries, and when to seek help. Each image corresponds to a major scene or turning point in the story.

---

## Image Requirements & Descriptions

### **a1.jpg** - SCENE 1: School Exit & Stranger Approach
**Order**: Scene introduction
**Context**: School gate, afternoon dismissal
**What to show**:
- A 9-year-old boy (Arav) exiting school
- An unfamiliar adult man approaching him
- School building in background
- Other children and teachers visible (safety in numbers)
- Clear body language: Arav looks uncertain/hesitant

**Narrative purpose**: Establishes the crisis moment. Visual should show initial approach by stranger.
**Emotional tone**: Safe, educational, slightly tense but not scary

**Dimensions**: 
- Recommended: 736x388px (matches current layout)
- Format: JPG, optimized for web

---

### **a2.jpg** - SCENE 2: Multiple Branching Paths

## Design Specifications

### Image Style
- **Tone**: Age-appropriate, safe, illustrated (not photorealistic trauma images)
- **Cultural context**: Indian setting (school uniforms, architecture, street scenes)
- **Diversity**: Show diverse trusted adults, children from different backgrounds
- **Accessibility**: Use clear colors, avoid flashing/strobe effects

### Technical Specs
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Size**: 736x388px (for quiz card display)
- **Color space**: RGB
- **Optimization**: Compressed for web (<100KB each recommended)

### Content Warnings
- Images should be educational, NOT traumatizing
- Show safe outcomes more than dangers
- Focus on empowerment and support
- NO graphic abuse, violence, or sexual content
- NO victim-blaming visuals

---

## Alternative: Symbolic/Diagram Approach

If photorealistic images are not feasible, use:
- **Simplified illustrations**: Stick figures, cartoon characters
- **Icons and diagrams**: Safety flowcharts, decision trees
- **Infographics**: Visual representations of concepts
- **Split screens**: Show safe vs. unsafe sides
- **Timeline format**: Sequential panels showing story progression
